Each copy is a place a fix can miss. Found by the 2026-10-11 de-bloat sweep (#96 row 2a) at `927bc53`. No refusal text, event shape or verdict changes.

**Evidence, one helper each:**
1. **Runs of a ticket** (scan `runs/*/meta.yaml` by ticket, role, finished): `compose.py:16-26` (`_runs_for`), `compose.py:413`, `specstore.py:274-283` (`critic_rounds`), `cli.py:1725-1734` (`_parent_close_verified`, which re-implements `_runs_for`; covered by `test_parent_close_reuse`), `store.py:135-139` (`clear_scratch`).
